## NVIDIA Vera CPUs on OCI
**Published:** October 27, 2026 · 
[Read Oracle's announcement](https://blogs.oracle.com/cloud-infrastructure/nvidia-vera-cpus-oci)
- **What it describes:** OCI Compute with NVIDIA Vera CPUs, including bare-metal and Flex VM options, for CPU-intensive services around AI systems.
- **When it is relevant:** Evaluating agent orchestration, tool/code execution, retrieval, and other CPU and memory-intensive AI application components.
- **Availability note:** The article is titled “Announcing General Availability,” while its closing paragraph says availability in select regions this quarter. Confirm current shape, region, and capacity details before making a deployment commitment.
- **Performance note:** The article identifies its cited performance comparison as NVIDIA platform benchmarking rather than OCI instance-level testing. Validate your own workload; this collection makes no blanket performance or savings claim.
